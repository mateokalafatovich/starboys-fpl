Background

In early 2026, five of the current Starboys were playing a game known as footy impostor. It is a version of the game imposter, but oriented around soccer players. There is a soccer player chosen at random for the round. Each person is then assigned a role: civilian or imposter. The civilians know the name of the soccer player, while the 1 imposter does not. The goal of the game for a civilian is to not give away the soccer player to the imposter, while also reassuring the rest of the civilians that they are not the imposter. Each person goes around saying a word, and after each round, everyone votes who they think is the imposter, and that person is eliminated, or until the imposter is the last of two people standing.

Roles were assigned. The player of the round was Bukayo Saka , also known as 🌟Starboy🌟. Juan goes first and says a seemingly unsuspicious word. Then comes Cross. Of all the many things there are to say about Bukayo Saka: washed, finished, unc, Cross says "STARBOY". The one phrase Bukayo Saka is the owner of in the soccer world. Everyone burst out laughing. Cross had completely ruined the round and given it away to the imposter, but we all couldn't help but laugh at the shockingly atrocious assemblance of words Cross chose to utter.

To this day, the Starboy incident is still remembered among the Starboys present that day as the worst imposter word ever uttered by a civilian in a game of imposter. In honor of Cross' performance and in light of the meme Saka has become in the footy world, the Starboys have named their FPL League after the Arsenal number 7.

Today

Today the Starboys compete for a grand prize pool of $500. The winner gets glory and bragging rights for at least a year, while the loser... is in for a rough time. This year's punishment will include a devastatingly dreadful TikTok which the last place will have to post online for thousands of people to see.
